01. Introduction

Q-Cubic is a technology and creative studio brand. This website introduces its services and provides a way to make enquiries. The contracting person or entity must be identified in the project agreement before an engagement is accepted.

02. Website use and acceptance

Please read these draft terms before using this website. Do not use it unlawfully, interfere with its operation, or attempt unauthorised access. A project is not formed merely by browsing or sending an enquiry; acceptance and any binding terms must be recorded in the applicable agreement.

03. Services

Q-Cubic offers software and website development, applications, AI services, automation, creative services, animation, gaming, video production and robotics-related work. Website descriptions are illustrative; the agreed scope defines the service actually purchased.

04. Project agreements

A project is governed by its specific proposal, quotation, statement of work, agreement or invoice, as accepted by the parties. A signed project agreement takes precedence over conflicting general website terms. Delivery assumptions, client responsibilities and acceptance criteria should be set out there.

05. Pricing and payments

Website prices are starting estimates. A quotation states fees, currency, taxes, third-party costs, advance payments, milestones and due dates. Payment schedules, cancellation charges and refunds must be expressly agreed; this page does not establish a universal non-refundable deposit. Any late-payment charge or suspension must follow the project agreement and applicable law.

06. Scope changes

Additional features, deliverables or changes after approval may require a revised quotation and timeline. Work on a change should begin only after both parties confirm its scope and commercial effect in writing.

07. Revisions and acceptance

The project agreement defines included revision rounds, feedback windows and acceptance criteria. Requests outside that allowance may be separately quoted. Clients should consolidate feedback and supply agreed materials on time.

08. Intellectual property

Ownership and licensing of final deliverables are determined by the project agreement and payment status. Pre-existing tools, frameworks, reusable code, internal processes, templates and generic components remain with their respective owners unless expressly agreed otherwise. Third-party materials remain subject to their licences. Any portfolio use of client work must be agreed.

09. Third-party services

Hosting, domains, APIs, AI platforms, payment providers, stock assets, fonts, plugins and cloud services may have independent terms, fees and limits. The proposal should identify material dependencies, who holds accounts, and who pays recurring costs.

10. AI-generated content

AI may assist image, video, voice, code, automation and creative development. Provider terms and output limitations may apply. AI outputs can contain errors, resemble existing material or require further clearance; the agreement should define review responsibilities and permitted uses. No promise of exclusive ownership in every AI output is made here.

11. Client content

Clients should provide only images, logos, videos, text, trademarks and data they are authorised to use and disclose. A project agreement should grant the permissions reasonably needed to perform the work. Do not send confidential or personal data that is unnecessary for the enquiry.

12. Confidentiality

Confidential information is to be handled under the agreed confidentiality terms and applicable law. Any special restrictions on AI providers, subcontractors or external processing should be agreed before information is shared.

13. Website availability

The website may be changed, maintained or temporarily unavailable. Information is provided for general evaluation and may need confirmation in a current proposal. No uninterrupted availability is promised.

14. Liability

Any project-specific allocation or limit of liability must be expressly agreed and legally reviewed. Nothing here excludes liability or statutory rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. This draft does not set a monetary cap or waive mandatory consumer protections.

15. Claims and indemnities

Clients remain responsible for the rights in materials they supply and for unlawful misuse of deliverables. Any indemnity for third-party claims must be expressly defined in the project agreement, including notice, defence and settlement procedures, and remain subject to applicable law.

16. Termination

The project agreement should specify termination rights, notice, opportunities to remedy a breach, fees for completed work, return of materials and handover obligations. No automatic forfeiture of payments is created by this draft.

17. Governing law and jurisdiction

The appropriate governing law, dispute procedure and court jurisdiction will be confirmed once the contracting party and project arrangements are identified. Jurisdiction: to be confirmed.

18. Changes

This draft may be updated before publication. Final website terms should show their effective date. Changes to website wording do not silently amend an existing signed project agreement.
