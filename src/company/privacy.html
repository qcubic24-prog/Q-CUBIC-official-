01. Who we are

This notice concerns the Q-Cubic website and enquiries made to Q-Cubic. The registered entity and final responsible-party details remain to be confirmed. Contact: contact@q-cubic.com.

02. Information you choose to provide

An enquiry may contain your name, email, phone number, company, project requirements and files you choose to send. Career enquiries may include a resume, portfolio, education, work history and contact details. Send only information relevant to your enquiry; identity documents, financial credentials and sensitive records are not requested.

03. Technical information and external media

This version has no enquiry form, account registration, payment checkout, newsletter subscription or analytics integration. Contact buttons open your email application; clicking alone does not send an enquiry. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and some media from CloudFront. Those requests expose connection information such as an IP address and browser headers to the providers. Hosting services may also process access logs; production hosting and log-retention settings must be confirmed before launch.

04. Purposes

Information provided by email is used to respond to the enquiry, discuss requirements, prepare a quotation, arrange a project or consider a career expression of interest. Project delivery, security and legal recordkeeping may require further processing, which should be explained in the relevant engagement. Enquiry details should not be repurposed for unrelated marketing without an appropriate basis.

05. Consent and choices

Where consent is required, the final notice should specify the information and purpose before collection. Do not treat visiting the site as blanket permission for unrelated uses. You can choose not to email Q-Cubic or can omit optional details, although this may limit the response.

06. Withdrawal

To withdraw consent where processing relies on it, email contact@q-cubic.com with the subject “Privacy request”. State the activity you want stopped. Withdrawal may affect services that depend on that information, but does not by itself invalidate earlier lawful processing. Any legal retention obligation should be explained in the response.

07. Sharing and service providers

Email is handled through the configured email provider. Project tools, cloud services or payment services should receive information only where needed for an agreed purpose and subject to appropriate terms. The final provider inventory and actual sharing practices must be confirmed before this draft is published. This notice does not claim that every category of provider is currently in use.

08. Security

The final operating policy should provide access controls, secure accounts, limited access and appropriate technical and organisational safeguards. Specific controls have not been audited as part of this website build. Avoid sending passwords or sensitive material by ordinary email; agree a suitable transfer method first.

09. Retention

The proposed policy is to retain personal data only as long as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose or as required by applicable law. Specific enquiry, recruitment, project and backup retention periods must be agreed before publication. You may request deletion; any information that must be retained should be identified in the response.

10. Cookies and local storage

No first-party tracking cookies, analytics scripts or persistent visitor preferences are set by the current site code. Third-party font/media providers and the eventual hosting configuration may process technical data under their own practices. Reassess this notice and any consent requirements before adding analytics, advertising, forms or embedded services.

11. Requests and applicable rights

You may contact Q-Cubic about access, correction, updating, deletion, consent withdrawal or a grievance. Any statutory rights, including nomination where applicable, depend on the law in force and its requirements. Identity may need to be verified proportionately. India’s DPDP framework has phased commencement; this draft does not claim every provision is already in force.

12. Children

This website is intended for professional enquiries. It does not intentionally seek personal data from children. If information about a child has been sent without appropriate authorisation, contact Q-Cubic so the matter can be assessed. A service involving children requires separate review and any legally required parental consent.

13. Third-party websites

Following external links or using your email service brings their policies into play. Q-Cubic does not control those services. Review their notices before sharing personal information.

14. International processing

Email, fonts, content delivery and future project services may involve infrastructure outside India. The actual provider locations, contractual protections and applicable transfer restrictions must be checked before the final policy is published.

15. Updates and grievances

Send privacy questions or complaints to contact@q-cubic.com. No Data Protection Officer is represented as appointed. The final notice should identify the responsible contact and applicable response procedure. Updates should carry a revision date and explain material changes.

Legal reference: MeitY — Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025. The rules provide phased commencement. This draft requires legal review and verification against Q-Cubic’s actual operations before publication.
